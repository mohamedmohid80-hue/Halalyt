# hadi.teachings: Thumbnail Guide

Built from a detailed breakdown of 6 high-performing thumbnails (Islamic and mystery-commentary niches). Measurements were taken on each image: brightness (0–255), contrast (spread of light/dark), saturation and where the visual weight sits.

## 2. The formula they all share

1. **One clear subject**, big, usually **cut out** with an outline or glow, and often **overlapping the text** for depth.
2. **The subject is the most saturated, brightest thing.** The background is darkened, desaturated or blurred to push it forward.
3. **Background = context.** Either one blurred photo, or a collage of 3–4 panels where each panel is a piece of the story.
4. **2–5 words of text, giant.** Heavy sans-serif (Anton / Helvetica Black / Impact style), white or yellow, with a black edge or drop shadow. **Red underline or red box** on the key line.
5. **A limited palette:** one dominant hue, plus white, plus one accent (red or yellow).
6. **A curiosity device**, always at least one:
   - an incomplete thought (ellipsis, quotes, "the reality of…")
   - unreadable or hidden information (Hebrew word, blurred figure)
   - a progression with gaps (stage 1 → 4 → 9)
   - a **juxtaposition** (cartoon vs reality, holy site vs threat)
7. **Annotations**: a red circle, an arrow, or an evidence-style label pointing at one specific detail.
8. **Recognition**: a famous character, place, person or object understood in a split second.
9. **Real imagery and texture**: photos, manga art, scanlines, grain, photo prints. **No flat vector clip-art as the main subject.**
10. **Readable at phone size**: at most 3 big elements; it should still work at 320×180.

## 3. What this means for hadi.teachings

- **Hadi is the channel's face, not always the hero.** Use him as a **reaction element** (corner, smaller, white outline), while the topic's real image is the hero.
- The **hero image should be real and recognisable**: the game, the app, the thing being discussed, cut out with a white sticker outline.
- **Juxtaposition is our strongest tool**: the topic versus an Islamic symbol (prayer mat, mosque, Quran), split or side by side.
- Keep **text to 2–4 words** that *add* to the title, not repeat it.
- Always add **one annotation** (circle, arrow or stamp) and **one curiosity device**.
- Finish with **texture** (grain, light scanlines, vignette) so it doesn't look flat or AI-generated.

## 4. Checklist before publishing

- [ ] One hero subject, the most saturated thing in the frame
- [ ] Background darkened, blurred or desaturated
- [ ] 2–4 words, giant, outlined, high contrast
- [ ] One red accent (box, underline, circle or arrow)
- [ ] One curiosity device (gap, mystery, juxtaposition)
- [ ] Something recognisable within half a second
- [ ] Texture or vignette, no flat clip-art look
- [ ] Still readable at 320×180
- [ ] Nothing inappropriate visible (blur it), and nothing that mocks Islam
