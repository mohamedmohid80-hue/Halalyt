## 1. HOOK

```
Many of you already know that GTA 6 just came out, and people are going crazy over it.

Millions of people waited years for this game. And a lot of them are Muslims.

But what if I told you that most Muslims buying this game have no idea what they're actually letting into their homes?

Now, you might be thinking, it's just a game. What does this have to do with Islam?

Well, a lot more than you think. Let me explain.
```

---

## 2. WHAT YOU'RE ACTUALLY DOING IN THIS GAME

```
Think about what GTA actually is.

You don't just watch crime. You become the criminal.

You steal cars. You rob stores. You shoot people. You sell drugs. And the game rewards you for it. The worse you behave, the more money you make, and the more fun it feels.

Now ask yourself one question. If these were real sins in real life, would you be laughing?

Because that's the danger. It's not just about one mission. It's about what happens to your heart when sin starts to feel normal. When something Allah hates becomes something you enjoy.
```

---

## 3. REASON ONE: WHAT YOUR EYES SEE

```
Reason number one. What it puts in front of your eyes.

GTA has always been known for its strip clubs, nudity, and scenes no Muslim should be watching. And you don't even have to go looking for it. It's built into the world you're walking through.

Allah says in the Quran.
```

> 🔊 **RECITATION R1: Surah An-Nur 24:30**

```
"Tell the believing men to lower their gaze and guard their chastity. That is purer for them. Indeed, Allah is fully aware of what they do."

That's Surah An-Nur, verse thirty.

And the Prophet, sallallahu alayhi wa sallam, said that the zina of the eyes is looking. That's in Sahih al-Bukhari and Sahih Muslim.

So when you play for hours, it's not just a screen. It's your eyes. And your eyes will be asked about what they looked at.
```

---

## 4. REASON TWO: IT MAKES MAJOR SINS FUN

```
Reason number two. It turns major sins into entertainment.

Drinking. Drugs. Gambling in casinos. Killing innocent people for fun.

These aren't small things in Islam. Allah says.
```

> 🔊 **RECITATION R2: Surah Al-Ma'idah 5:90**

```
"O you who believe, intoxicants, gambling, idols and divining arrows are only filth from the work of Shaytan, so avoid it, so that you may be successful."

That's Surah Al-Ma'idah, verse ninety.

Allah didn't just say don't do it. He said, avoid it. Stay away from it.

And Allah describes the true believers as those who do not witness falsehood. That's in Surah Al-Furqan, verse seventy-two.

So how can we stay away from something... that we spend our free time playing?
```

---

## 5. REASON THREE: YOUR TIME

```
Reason number three. Your time.

The Prophet, sallallahu alayhi wa sallam, said: "There are two blessings that many people lose out on. Good health and free time." That's in Sahih al-Bukhari.

And he told us that on the Day of Judgement, our feet will not move until we are asked about our life, and how we spent it. That's in Jami' at-Tirmidhi.

Now think about it. Two hours a day. Fourteen hours a week. Hundreds of hours a year.

That's time you could have spent with your family. Learning your deen. Building something real.

Your time is your life. And GTA is designed to take as much of it as possible.
```

---

## 6. HOW THE DUNYA CONTROLS US

```
But wait. There's a bigger picture here.

Look at how the world reacted to this game. The trailers broke records. Millions of people counted down the days. People queued at midnight like it was the most important day of their lives.

This is how the dunya controls people. It doesn't force you. It excites you. It keeps you busy. It keeps you entertained. So you never stop to think about what really matters.

Allah described this exact thing over fourteen hundred years ago.
```

> 🔊 **RECITATION R3: Surah Al-Hadid 57:20**

```
"Know that the life of this world is only play and amusement, adornment, boasting among yourselves, and competing in wealth and children."

That's Surah Al-Hadid, verse twenty.

Play and amusement. That's what the dunya is. And the people who make these games know exactly how to keep you hooked. The rewards, the missions, the online updates. It's built so you never feel like stopping.

The question is, are you using the dunya? Or is the dunya using you?
```

---

## 7. "BUT IT'S JUST A GAME"

```
Now some people will say, "Bro, relax. It's just a game. I know it's not real."

But that's exactly how it works. You don't fall into sin all at once. It starts with laughing at it. Then getting used to it. Then not feeling anything when you see it.

And this doesn't mean every video game is haram. Scholars differ on games in general, and games without haram content are a different discussion. Ask a scholar you trust.

But a game built around nudity, killing, drugs and gambling? That's not a grey area.
```

---

## 8. MERCY

```
Now if you're watching this and you've already bought it, or you've played the older ones for years, don't panic.

Allah is the Most Forgiving. The fact that this video reached you might be Allah giving you a way out.

Delete it. Replace it with something better. Play halal games. Go to the gym. Spend that time with your family. Read even one page of the Quran a day.

What you leave for the sake of Allah, Allah will replace with something better.
```
