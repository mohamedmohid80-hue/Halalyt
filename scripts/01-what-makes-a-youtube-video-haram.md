## 1. LYING FOR VIEWS (0:35–1:25)

**ON SCREEN:** Big **1** pops, then sticker **LYING FOR VIEWS** (red). Cascade of 3 fake clickbait thumbnails (made by us, no real channels) with exaggerated titles. On "nothing happens" → Hadi `facepalm`. Hadith cards: clean card, Hadi off screen, source + grade at bottom. On "change the title" → a fake title gets crossed out and rewritten (scribble), green ✓ stamps.
**SFX:** pop · whoosh ×3 · scribble · thud (✓)

**VOICEOVER**
```
Number one. Lying for views.

You've seen these titles. "You won't BELIEVE what happened." "He did THIS in the masjid." And then you click... and nothing happens.

Or worse, the whole story is made up.

The Prophet, sallallahu alayhi wa sallam, said: "Woe to the one who speaks and lies to make people laugh. Woe to him, woe to him."

And he also said: "Whoever cheats is not one of us."

Now think about it. If lying to make people laugh is that serious... what about lying to get millions of views?

The fix is simple. Your title can be exciting. It can make people curious. But it has to be true. If your video doesn't deliver what the title promises... change the title.
```

```
Now, comedy itself isn't haram. You can be funny. Just never make Allah, His Messenger, or the deen the punchline.
```

**VOICEOVER**

```
Number three. Spreading fake hadith.
```

**SFX:** pop · whoosh · thud ×3 (✕)

**VOICEOVER**
```
Number five. Showing or promoting haram.

This includes thumbnails with inappropriate images, just to get clicks.

And it includes sponsors. Betting apps. Gambling sites. Loans built on interest.

Some of these sponsors pay really well. And that's exactly the test.

Allah warns that those who love to see indecency spread among the believers will have a painful punishment, in this life and the next. That's Surah An-Nur, verse nineteen.

So here's a simple rule. If you wouldn't want your parents to see your thumbnail... it probably shouldn't be your thumbnail. And if a sponsor is selling something haram, the money isn't worth it.
```

**SFX:** pop · whoosh · pop ×3

**VOICEOVER**
```
Number six. Stealing other people's work.

Re-uploading someone else's video. Taking a scholar's lecture, cutting it up and monetising it. Using a reciter's recitation without permission.

A lot of people think, "It's Islamic content, so it's fine."

But someone worked hard on that. Many scholars recognise that creators have rights over their work. And taking it without permission can mean taking something that isn't yours.

So ask for permission. Credit the source. Or make your own.
```

```
And here's the beautiful part. The same hadith also says: "Whoever starts a good practice in Islam gets its reward, and the reward of everyone who acts on it after him."

So the exact same algorithm that can multiply your sins... can multiply your good deeds. Every person who learns something true from your video. Every person who shares it. That's reward that keeps coming.
```
