## 1. HOOK

```
Many of you have seen these posts. A verse from the Quran, a beautiful recitation, and then the caption.

"Share this with ten people and Allah will open a door for you tonight."
"If you scroll past this, Shaytan stopped you."
"Don't skip. Allah sent this to you for a reason."

And millions of Muslims share them. Because who would scroll past the Quran?

But what if I told you that a lot of these posts aren't really about the Quran at all?

Now, sharing the Quran is a beautiful thing. That's not the problem. The problem is what they attach to it. Let me explain.
```

---

## 2. POINT ONE: THEY GUILT TRIP YOU

```
Number one. They guilt trip you.

"If you scroll past this, Shaytan stopped you."
"Only true believers will share this."
"Let's see how many people really love Allah."

Read that again. They're telling you that if you don't press share, something is wrong with your iman.

But who gave them the right to say that? How do they know why you scrolled past? How do they know what Shaytan did or didn't do?

Allah says in the Quran.
```

> 📖 ON SCREEN: Arabic of An-Naml 27:65 + reference

```
"Say, none in the heavens and the earth knows the unseen except Allah."

Only Allah knows what's in your heart. Not a page with a million followers. Your love for Allah is not measured by a share button.
```

---

## 3. POINT TWO: FAKE PROMISES

```
Number two. Fake promises.

"Share this and you'll hear good news within twenty-four hours."
"Send this to seven people and your problems will be solved."
"Ignore this and you'll have bad luck for a year."

Where did they get that from? Not from the Quran. Not from the Prophet, sallallahu alayhi wa sallam.

Allah lists the things He has forbidden, and among them is this: that you say about Allah what you do not know.

And Allah warns against making up rules with your tongue and lying about Allah.

Promising a reward that Allah never promised is not a small thing. It's speaking on Allah's behalf. And these pages do it every single day, with a time limit attached, so you don't stop to think.
```

---

## 4. POINT THREE: USING YOUR FEAR OF ALLAH FOR VIEWS AND MONEY

```
Number three. They use your fear of Allah to get views and money.

Think about how these posts work. A Muslim sees the Quran. They feel scared to scroll past. So they share, comment and type Ameen. The post goes viral. The page grows.

And then what?

A lot of these pages make money from those views. Some of them start selling things. Some of them get sold to someone else completely, with all those followers you helped build.

Your fear of Allah, the most sacred feeling a Muslim has, turned into numbers on someone's dashboard.

Allah says to the people who traded His words for worldly gain.
```

> 📖 ON SCREEN: Arabic of Al-Baqarah 2:41 + reference

```
"And do not exchange My verses for a small price, and fear only Me."

And the Prophet, sallallahu alayhi wa sallam, told us that among the first people judged on the Day of Judgement is a man who recited the Quran. But he didn't do it for Allah. He did it so people would say, he's a reciter.

If reciting the Quran for praise is that serious, what about using the Quran for views?

Now, to be clear, not every page that posts Quran is doing this. Many people share it sincerely, and may Allah reward them. But when the Quran comes with pressure, threats and fake promises, ask yourself who that post is really serving.
```

---

## 5. WHAT TO DO INSTEAD

```
So what should you do?

Share the Quran because you love it, not because someone scared you into it.

If a post threatens you, guilt trips you, or promises you something Allah never promised, don't share that version. Find the same verse from a sincere source and share it with your own words.

And if you really want the reward, don't just share the verse. Read it. Understand it. Live it.
```

---

## 6. MERCY

```
Now if you've shared these posts before, don't panic. Most of us have. You did it out of love for Allah, and Allah knows your intention.

But now that you know, you know.
```
