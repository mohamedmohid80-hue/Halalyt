TITLE
How to Earn 1000s of Good Deeds Every Day

DESCRIPTION
The Prophet ﷺ once asked his Companions: "Is any one of you unable to earn a thousand good deeds every day?" In this video we go through seven simple acts from authentic hadith that can earn you thousands of good deeds every single day. They also wipe away your sins and raise your status with Allah, and most of them take just a few minutes or are things you already do.

📖 SOURCES
• "Is any one of you unable to earn a thousand good deeds every day?" / 100 SubhanAllah: Sahih Muslim 2698
• Good deeds written as ten, up to 700 times and more: Sahih al-Bukhari 6491, Sahih Muslim 131
• One salawat: ten blessings, ten sins wiped, raised ten levels: Sunan an-Nasa'i 1297, Sahih Muslim 408
• Every letter of the Quran is a good deed, multiplied by ten: Jami' at-Tirmidhi 2910
• Surah al-Ikhlas is equal to a third of the Quran: Sahih al-Bukhari 5013, Sahih Muslim 811
• La ilaha illallah wahdahu... 100 times: Sahih al-Bukhari 3293, Sahih Muslim 2691
• Prayer in congregation is 27 times better: Sahih al-Bukhari 645, Sahih Muslim 650
• Isha and Fajr in congregation, like praying the whole night: Sahih Muslim 656
• Every step to the masjid wipes a sin and raises a level: Sahih Muslim 666

The numbers in the daily total are approximate (letter counts differ slightly between scholars). Allah knows best.
