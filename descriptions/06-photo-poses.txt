TITLE
Muslims, Stop Doing These Poses in Photos

DESCRIPTION
The peace sign, the devil horns, the "I love you" sign, the OK sign. Everyone does them in pictures, but most people have no idea where they come from. Some have a history you'd want to stay away from, and some are just myths. In this video we go through each pose, where it actually comes from, and which ones a Muslim should avoid.

Hadith

Rulings
• "V (Victory Sign) Has Nothing to Do with Satan": Islamweb, Fatwa No. 398227

History
• The V sign: Victor de Laveleye's BBC broadcast, January 1941, later popularised by Winston Churchill
• The sign of the horns: the Italian "corna" against the evil eye, made famous by Ronnie James Dio
• The OK sign: listed by the Anti-Defamation League in 2019 after a 2017 online hoax

This video isn't about judging anyone in old photos. Most people make these poses without knowing what they mean. Allah knows best.
