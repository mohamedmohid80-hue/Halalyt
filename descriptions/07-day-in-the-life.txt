TITLE
A Day in the Life of Muslims 1400 Years Ago

DESCRIPTION
No alarm clock, no electricity, no fridge, no phone. So what did a normal day actually look like for Muslims in Madinah, in the time of the Prophet ﷺ? From waking up for Fajr in the dark to going to sleep right after Isha, we go through the whole day using authentic hadith, and look at what their day had that most of us are missing.

📖 SOURCES
• Women returning from Fajr unrecognised in the dark: Sahih al-Bukhari 578, Sahih Muslim 645
• Sitting after Fajr until sunrise, talking and laughing: Sahih Muslim 670
• Months with no fire lit, living on dates and water: Sahih al-Bukhari 6459, Sahih Muslim 2972
• "Show me the market" (Abdur-Rahman ibn Awf): Sahih al-Bukhari 2048
• Uthman and the well of Rumah: Jami' at-Tirmidhi 3703
• Qaylulah and lunch after Jumuah: Sahih al-Bukhari 939
• The Prophet's rooms of mud brick and palm branches: al-Adab al-Mufrad 450
• Horse racing: Sahih al-Bukhari 420
• "Shoot, and I am with you": Sahih al-Bukhari 2899
• Houses had no lamps: Sahih al-Bukhari 382
• Disliking sleep before Isha and talking after it: Sahih al-Bukhari 568
