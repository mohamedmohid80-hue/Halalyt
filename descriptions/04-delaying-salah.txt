TITLE
Is It Okay to Pray When You Get Home?

DESCRIPTION
Dhuhr comes in at 1:15, but you've got class until 3. So you tell yourself, I'll pray when I get home. Almost every Muslim at school or work has done this. But is it actually okay?

In this video we go through how prayer times really work, what the Quran and Sunnah say about delaying salah, what counts as a real excuse, when combining Dhuhr and Asr is allowed, and simple ways to pray on time at school or at work.

📖 SOURCES
Quran
• Prayer at fixed times: Surah An-Nisa 4:103
• "Woe to those who pray, but are heedless of their prayer": Surah Al-Ma'un 107:4–5

Hadith
• The most beloved deed is the prayer at its time: Sahih al-Bukhari 527, Sahih Muslim 85
• Whoever misses Asr, it is as if he lost his family and wealth: Sahih al-Bukhari 552, Sahih Muslim 626
• The time of Asr lasts until the sun turns yellow: Sahih Muslim 612
• Whoever sleeps through or forgets a prayer should pray it when he remembers: Sahih al-Bukhari 597, Sahih Muslim 684
• Asr missed during the Battle of the Trench: Sahih al-Bukhari 596
• Ibn Abbas: the Prophet ﷺ combined Dhuhr and Asr in Madinah without travel, fear or rain: Sahih Muslim 705
• The whole earth has been made a place of prayer: Sahih al-Bukhari 335
• Wiping over khuffs, a day and a night: Sahih Muslim 276

Scholars differ on the details of combining prayers. Ask a scholar you trust about your own situation. Allah knows best.
