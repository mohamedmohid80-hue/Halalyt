# Research: "Don't do these poses in pictures"

Angle: a teaching video that sorts the poses into **avoid / depends on context / harmless (myth)**, instead of the usual conspiracy lists. Built on one Islamic principle, explained properly.

## The Islamic principle
- Imitating non-Muslims (*tashabbuh*) is about things that are **distinctively theirs**: their acts of worship, religious symbols and identity markers. It is not about everything non-Muslims happen to do (IslamQA 45200).
- "Whoever imitates a people is one of them" (Sunan Abi Dawud 4031). Check the grading before quoting; it's widely graded hasan.
- Actions are judged by intentions (Sahih al-Bukhari 1). Don't accuse people who made a pose innocently.
- Some scholars advise avoiding gestures that cause doubt or misunderstanding, because a hand sign can mean different things in different places (SeekersGuidance).
- No fatwa was found on the peace sign or the devil horns specifically. They're judged under the general principle, and scholars' views vary.

## The poses
| Pose | What it actually is | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| Devil horns (index + pinky up, **thumb tucked**) | Italian *corna*, a folk gesture to ward off or give the evil eye; made famous by Ronnie James Dio in metal. Dio himself said "it's NOT the devil's sign". Also used by MS-13 as a gang sign. | **Avoid.** The associations are the evil eye (superstition), "the devil" in pop culture, and gangs. A Muslim gains nothing from it. |
| Spider-Man web pose | Same hand shape as the horns (thumb tucked). | **Avoid** for the same reason, even if you mean Spider-Man. |
| ILY 🤟 (thumb **out**) | American Sign Language "I love you": the letters I, L and Y combined. | **Harmless.** It's often mistaken for the horns; the difference is the thumb. |
| OK sign 👌 | Means "OK" for almost everyone. A 2019 4chan hoax tied it to "white power"; the ADL listed it, and the Christchurch mosque attacker flashed it in court. | **Depends on context.** Fine as "OK". Avoid the upside-down "circle game" version and anything mocking. |
| Peace / V sign ✌️ | V for victory (WWII) and the 1960s peace sign; now a normal photo pose, especially in East Asia. The "broken cross / anti-Christian" story is a myth. | **Harmless.** Myth busted. |
| Covering one eye / triangle or diamond hands | Internet "Illuminati" conspiracy. The diamond is a record label's logo (Roc-A-Fella). | **Myth.** There's no religious meaning, but as a "cool" pose it's copying conspiracy culture for nothing. |
| Raising the index finger ☝️ | In Muslim culture, the sign of tawhid (pointing to the oneness of Allah), as in the tashahhud. | **The good one.** End the video on it. |
