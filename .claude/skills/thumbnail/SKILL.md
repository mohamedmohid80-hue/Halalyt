# hadi.teachings thumbnail skill

Built from a part-by-part breakdown of 6 viral thumbnails in the Islamic explainer niche (saved in `references/`), plus the earlier lessons in `thumbnails/THUMBNAIL-GUIDE.md`. Metrics were measured on each image at 320×180: brightness 0–255, contrast = spread of light/dark, saturation 0–255, "white %" = share of near-white pixels.

## 1. The six references, part by part

### 01 · "STAGE 1 / STAGE 5" (the grave)
| Part | Detail |
|---|---|
| Layout | **Two-panel split** with a thick black divider. Same scene in both, one thing changed. |
| Text | Only **"STAGE 1" / "STAGE 5"**: white rounded heavy font, thick black outline, top of each panel. No title words. |
| Subject | A simple **cartoon Muslim character** (red fez, white round head) in a kafan, lying in a cross-section of a grave. |

| Curiosity gap | **Stages 2, 3, 4 are missing.** The viewer clicks to fill the gap. The "OR" asks "which one will I get?" |
| Metrics | Bright 119 · contrast 80 · sat 148. Sky-blue top band, brown earth bottom: a **natural light-top/dark-bottom** frame. |

## 2. What the winners have in common

**Five proven formats** (pick one per thumbnail):

| Format | Refs | When to use | Key rule |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A. Stage / Level split** | 01, 04 | Progressions: the grave, iman levels, addiction → recovery, the dunya's grip | Two panels, numbers with a **gap** (1 → 5, 1 → 7), the same character, **opposite emotions**, red vs green or dark vs light |
| **B. Character + side note** | 03, 05 | One person or figure (Dajjal, Mahdi, a Prophet's story with no depiction, Shaytan) | Figure on the left (face or **from behind**), **handwritten text + hand-drawn arrow** on the right |
| **C. Clean comparison grid** | 02 | "X across…", "every type of…", lists | White background, 4–6 icons, handwritten labels, Islam first |
| **D. Dark mystery poster** | 06 | Exposés, idols, conspiracies, end-times | Black background, gold or grey subject, neon red title partly behind the subject |
| **E. Viewer-POV real photo** | (THUMBNAIL-GUIDE) | Practical topics: gold, food, phones, games | One real object held by a hand, blurred background, 2–3 huge words |

**Shared rules:**
1. **Text is tiny in word count.** 0–6 words. Three of the six have **no title phrase at all**: the image or the labels do the work.
2. **Fonts are friendly**: rounded heavy caps (stage/level) or **casual handwriting** (side notes, labels). No sharp corporate fonts. This is what makes the niche feel *personal* rather than news-like.
3. **One clear subject per panel**, big, with a clean edge (cartoon outline or cut-out).
4. **Emotion through faces or posture**: terror vs relief, sad vs smiling, a blind eye, a turned back.
5. **A curiosity gap is always present**: missing stages, an "OR", "I guess", "reject", "who is…".
6. **Colour carries meaning**: red = bad / punishment / haram, green = good / Jannah / deen, gold = sacred or idol, black = mystery.
7. **Two backgrounds dominate: pure white (62–70% white) or pure black (54–65% black).** Both read well at phone size. Avoid muddy mid-tones.
8. **Islamic signs from the texts** (كافر on the forehead, a blind eye, kafan, garden vs fire windows) give **instant recognition** to Muslim viewers.
9. **Respect:** the Prophet ﷺ, Sahaba and the Mahdi are **never shown with faces** (from behind, silhouettes or calligraphy only).

## 3. Metric targets (check with the measure script below)

| Format | Brightness | Contrast | Saturation | Background |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A. Stage/Level | 100–125 | ≥ 65 | 140–190 | Two strong colours, split |
| B. Character + note (white) | 180–200 | ≥ 80 | 30–60 | ≥ 55% white |
| B. Character + note (black) | 45–65 | ≥ 80 | ≤ 30 | ≥ 55% black |
| C. Grid | 210–230 | 50–65 | ≤ 30 | ≥ 65% white |
| D. Dark poster | 20–40 | 30–60 | 80–110 | ≥ 50% black |

Everything must still read at **320×180** (the size of a phone feed).

## 4. Our version: how hadi.teachings applies this

- **Our character is Hadi** (faceless, white kufi, green hoodie), not the fez character. Hadi has no face, so emotion comes from **pose, props, sweat drops, ? / ! bubbles and thought bubbles**. Use `brand/hadi/*.svg` and `tools/make_hadi.py` to make new poses.
- For people we must not depict (the Prophet ﷺ, Sahaba, the Mahdi), use **figures from behind**, silhouettes, or **calligraphy seals**.
- Text fonts: **rounded heavy caps** (e.g. Fredoka/Lilita One) for stage/level labels; a **casual handwriting** font (e.g. Patrick Hand / Kalam) for side notes and labels. White with black outline on colour; black (with one red word) on white.
- Never use explicit-site logos, real people in a defamatory context, or anything that mocks Islam. Blur anything inappropriate.
- **Our proven hit:** "Why These Islamic Videos Are Haram". Topics that call out something Muslims see every day work. Pair them with Format B or A.

## 5. Build workflow

1. **Pick the format** (A–E) from the topic using the table in §2.
2. **Write the text**: 0–6 words. It must **add** to the title, not repeat it. Make the surprising word the biggest.
3. **Pick the curiosity device**: a gap in the numbers, "OR", a hedge ("I guess"), a contradiction ("reject"), "who is…".
4. **Build at 1280×720** as HTML (rendered with Playwright) or SVG (rendered with cairosvg), the same as the existing thumbnails in `thumbnails/`.
5. **Measure** it against §3:
   ```bash
   python3 -I - thumb.png <<'EOF'
   import sys; from PIL import Image, ImageStat
   im=Image.open(sys.argv[1]).convert("RGB").resize((320,180)); L=im.convert("L")
   print("bright",round(ImageStat.Stat(L).mean[0]),"contrast",round(ImageStat.Stat(L).stddev[0]),
         "sat",round(ImageStat.Stat(im.convert("HSV")).mean[1]),
         "white%",round(sum(p>235 for p in L.getdata())/576),"black%",round(sum(p<20 for p in L.getdata())/576))
   EOF
   ```
6. **Check at 320×180**: is the subject clear, is the text readable, and is the emotion obvious?
7. Show the user **one** strong version (they prefer one confident design over several weak options), then iterate on their feedback.

## 6. Checklist

- [ ] One of formats A–E, chosen on purpose
- [ ] 0–6 words; the surprising word is the biggest
- [ ] A curiosity gap is visible
- [ ] One clear subject per panel, with emotion shown through pose/face/props
- [ ] Colours carry meaning (red bad, green good, gold sacred, black mystery)
- [ ] Clean white or black background (no muddy mid-tones), or a strong two-colour split
- [ ] Metrics within the §3 range for that format
- [ ] Readable at 320×180
- [ ] No faces for the Prophet ﷺ / Sahaba / Mahdi; no explicit logos; no defamatory use of real people
